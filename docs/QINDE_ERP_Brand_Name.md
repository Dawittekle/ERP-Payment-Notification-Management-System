# QINDE — ERP Brand Name

## Official Brand Name

**English:** QINDE  
**Amharic:** ቅንደ

## Meaning

**QINDE (ቅንደ)** is inspired by the Oromo root **qindaa-**, associated with **organizing, arranging, coordinating, and putting things in order**.

### Brand meaning

> **QINDE = Business, in order.**

The name represents the purpose of the ERP: bringing important business operations together and keeping them organized and coordinated.

## Usage Rules

Use **QINDE** as the primary product/brand name in code, documentation, UI, and agent instructions.

Preferred forms:

- **QINDE**
- **Qinde ERP**
- **Qinde Business Platform**

Do not rename the product to a payment-specific name. The platform covers broader financial/business operations.

## Project Context

QINDE is a production-style ERP financial operations platform covering:

- Identity and access management
- Customers
- Invoices
- Payments
- Refunds
- Notifications
- Audit
- Reporting
- Payment reconciliation

The payment workflow is important, but **QINDE is not only a payment system**.

## Agent Naming Guidance

When referring to the project, agents should use:

**Project:** QINDE ERP  
**Brand:** QINDE  
**Amharic:** ቅንደ  
**Tagline:** Business, in order.

Avoid introducing alternative product names unless explicitly requested.
